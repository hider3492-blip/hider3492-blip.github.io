Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Hutcrafta ("we", "our", or "us") is an offline puzzle game. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and disclosed when you play our mobile application.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not directly collect, store, or ask for any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location).

2. Third-Party Services and Ad Networks

The app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device for ad serving and analytics purposes. These service providers have their own privacy policies regarding data handling:

  • Google AdMob
  • Meta Audience Network
  • Unity Ads

3. GDPR, CCPA & CalOPPA Compliance

If you reside in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, Switzerland, or California, you have specific privacy rights regarding how ads are personalized. Consent choices for personalized advertising can be managed directly through the in-app privacy settings menu powered by Google User Messaging Platform (UMP).

4. Children’s Privacy

Our application is not targeted towards children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at hutcrafta@gmail.com.
